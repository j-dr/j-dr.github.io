Joseph DeRose

Assistant Scientist, Brookhaven National Laboratory

I study why the expansion of the universe is speeding up, and what that says about the fundamental building blocks of the Universe. I build the models, simulations and statistical tools that turn maps of millions of galaxies into precise tests of fundamental physics.

Currently

  • Combining DESI galaxy maps with weak gravitational lensing to measure how cosmic structure has grown over time.
  • Designing Steel: small, spectroscopically calibrated lensing samples that sidestep the largest systematic errors in weak lensing.
  • Developing effective field theory models of lensing that use more of the data without being biased by poorly understood small-scale astrophysics.

Collaborations

Much of my science is made possible by large international collaborations I'm part of:
