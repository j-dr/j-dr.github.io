Selected publications

A few recent highlights. For the full list, see Google Scholar, NASA ADS, ORCID or my CV.

  1. Steeling weak lensing source galaxy samples against systematics using wide field spectroscopy
    J. DeRose, N. Weaverdyck, M. White, S.-F. Chen, D. Schlegel, A. Slosar · 2026
    Sparse lensing samples give up almost no cosmological information, and are small enough to calibrate with spectra of a representative subset.
  2. Cosmic shear in effective field theory at two-loop order: revisiting S8 in Dark Energy Survey data
    S.-F. Chen, J. DeRose, M. M. Ivanov, O. H. E. Philcox · 2026
    DES lensing analyzed with first-principles theory shows no tension with the CMB, even with evolving dark energy.
  3. The lensing counter narrative: an effective description of small-scale clustering in weak lensing power spectra
    J. DeRose, S.-F. Chen · 2025
    A new way to use small-scale lensing data without being biased by uncertain astrophysics, with a public JAX pipeline.
  4. Simulation budgeting for hybrid effective field theories
    A. Bartlett, J. DeRose, M. White · JCAP 2026
    How many N-body simulations it takes to build percent-accurate emulators for future surveys.
  5. Evolution of structure growth during dark energy domination: insights from the cross-correlation of DESI galaxies with CMB lensing and galaxy magnification
    N. Sailer, J. DeRose, S. Ferraro, et al. · 2025
    Tracks how structure grew in the recent universe, including a growth measurement from galaxy magnification.
  6. Not all lensing is low: an analysis of DESI × DES using the Lagrangian effective theory of LSS
    S.-F. Chen, J. DeRose, R. Zhou, M. White, et al. · 2024
    DESI galaxies combined with DES lensing give a clustering amplitude consistent with the CMB.
  7. Aemulus ν: precise predictions for matter and biased tracer power spectra in the presence of neutrinos
    J. DeRose, N. Kokron, A. Banerjee, S.-F. Chen, M. White, R. Wechsler, et al. · 2023
    A simulation suite and public emulator for galaxy clustering and lensing predictions, including massive neutrinos.
  8. Precision redshift-space galaxy power spectra using Zel'dovich control variates
    J. DeRose, S.-F. Chen, N. Kokron, M. White · 2022
    Removes most of the statistical noise from simulation predictions at almost no extra cost.
